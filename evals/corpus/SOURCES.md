# Evaluation corpus sources

These five PDFs are stable snapshots of public U.S. government publications.
They are committed so evaluation runs do not silently change when an agency
updates a download URL. `manifest.json` records the official source, version,
page count, byte size, and SHA-256 checksum for every file.

The publications were obtained from NIST, CISA, the Federal Reserve Board, and
the White House Office of Management and Budget. Preserve their cover pages and
attribution. Before redistributing this corpus outside this repository, recheck
the source agency's current terms and any third-party material embedded in a
publication.

- `nist-ai-rmf-1.0.pdf`: NIST AI 100-1, January 26, 2023.
- `nist-csf-2.0.pdf`: NIST CSWP 29, February 26, 2024.
- `cisa-zero-trust-maturity-model-2.0.pdf`: CISA, April 2023.
- `federal-reserve-economic-well-being-2024.pdf`: Federal Reserve Board, May 2025.
- `omb-circular-a-123-2026.pdf`: OMB, March 10, 2026.
